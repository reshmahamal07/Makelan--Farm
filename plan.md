## 1. Goal and Must-Haves
The goal of this website is to introduce my business to customers and provide useful information.

### Website Requirements 
The website must have:
- Four HTmL pages.
- Clear navigation.
- Images with alt text.

## 2.Visitors
The website is designed for three types of visitors:
- **New customers:** People who want to learn about my business and the services it offers .
- **Returning customers:** People who want to find updated information about products ,services and opening hours.
- **Potential customer:** People who wnt to find contact information and ask questions.


## 3. Content Plan

### Home page (index.html)

Purpose: Introduce Mäkelän Farm to visitors.

Content:Welcome message,business introduction and pictures.

Next step: Visitors can learn more about my business.

### About Page (about.html)

Purpose: Tell vistors about my business.

Content: Business history, background and information.

Next step: Visitors can explore my services .

### Listing Page (listing.html)

Next step: Visitors can choose what they are interested in.

### Contact Page (contact.html)

Purpose: Help visitors contact my business.

Content: Email, phone number ,address and opening hours.

Next step: Visitors can contact me .

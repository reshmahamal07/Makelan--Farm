# Website Proposal – Mäkelän Farm Shop

Dear Mäkelän Farm Shop,

I would like to propose creating a new website for your farm shop. The purpose of the website is to introduce your business, show your products and help customers find useful information about your farm.

The following plan explains the website structure, visitors, content and what information I need from you.

I hope you will approve this proposal so I can start building the website.

Thank you!


## 1. Goal and Must-Haves
The goal of this website is to introduce Mäkelän Farm Shop to customers and provide useful information.

### Website Requirements 
The website must have:
- Four HTML pages.
- Clear navigation.
- Images with alt text.
- Clear headings and readable content.
- Working links between all pages.

## 2. Visitors
The website is designed for three types of visitors:
- **New customers:** People who want to learn about Mäkelän Farm Shop and the services it offers .
- **Returning customers:** People who want to find updated information about products, services and opening hours.
- **Potential customers:** People who want to find contact information and ask questions.


## 3. Content Plan

### Home page (index.html)

Purpose: Introduce Mäkelän Farm to visitors.

Content:Welcome message, business introduction and pictures.

Next step: Visitors can learn more about the farm.

### About Page (about.html)

Purpose: Tell visitors about Mäkelän Farm Shop.

Content: Business history, background and information.

Next step: Visitors can explore the products.

### Listing Page (listing.html)

Purpose: Show the products or services availableat the farm shop.

Content: Product descriptions, pictures and prices available.

Next step: Visitors can choose what they are interested in.

### Contact Page (contact.html)

Purpose: Help visitors contact Mäkelän Farm Shop.

Content: Email, phone number, address and opening hours.

Next step: Visitors can contact the farm shop or plan a visit .

### 4. Three real  sites 
I will look at three real farm shop or local food business websites for inspiration.


- Website 1: https://www.mattilafarm.com/
  What works: The website introduces the farm and its products.
  What does not work: Some information can make the website feel busy.
  What I will use: I will use clear product information and pictures.

- Website 2: https://www.uotilat.fi/english
  What works: The website gives information about the farm and its products.
  What does not work: Some sections have a lot of text.
  What I will use: I will make product information easy to find and understand.

- Website 3: https://maatilapuoti.fi/en/
  What works: The website provides information about products and the farm shop.

  What does not work: Visitors may need to look through different sections to find information.

  What I will use: I will make contact details and opening hours easy to find.

 ### 5. Tone 
 The website will have a friendly, welcoming and simple tone. The language will be easy to understand.

Three key messages:
- Welcome to Mäkelän Farm Shop.

- Discover fresh farm products.

- Contact us to learn more about our products.

### 6. What we need from you
To complete the website, I need:

- Photos of the farm and products.

- Information about the farm and its history.

- A list of available products and prices.

- Opening hours.

- Contact details and address.

Until I receive this information, I will use general text and temporary placeholders. I will not publish unconfirmed prices or opening hours.

### 7. Next Steps
After you approve this plan:
- I will create the four HTML pages.

- I will add the same header, navigation and footer to every page.

- I will add the content and images.

- I will check that all navigation links work.

- I will validate all HTML pages and fix any errors.

- I will publish the website using GitHub Pages.

- I will send you the website link for review.

Please let me know if you approve this plan or would like any changes.

Thank you!

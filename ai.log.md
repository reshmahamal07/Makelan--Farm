# AI Log – Mäkelän Farm Website

## 1. Planning my website

I used ChatGPT and Copilot to help me plan my Mäkelän Farm Shop website. I asked for ideas about which pages I should have and what information to include on each page.

ChatGPT and Copilot helped me organise my ideas and improve my website plan. I also used AI to check my progress after making commits.

## 2. Checking my plan

After writing my first plan, I asked ChatGPT to check if it was correct. It helped me find spelling mistakes and noticed that some page names in my plan did not match the actual pages on my website.

I used this feedback to understand what needed to be corrected.

## 3. Updating my website plan

I made changes to my `plan.md` file to improve my website proposal. I added more details about the website goals, visitors, page content, tone and next steps.

I also included three real farm shop websites as examples for inspiration. I used ChatGPT to help me organise the proposal and check the wording and structure.

After reviewing the suggestions, I updated my plan to make it clearer and more complete. I checked the changes myself to make sure they matched my website project.

## 4. Understanding the E1 assignment

I shared my issue in assignment to AI because I wanted to understand what I needed to do next.

It explained the HTML pages, navigation, headings, images and validation. It also explained the GitHub requirements.

This helped me understand the assignment better and plan the next steps.

## 5. Creating this AI log

I asked ChatGPT and Copilot what I should write in my `ai-log.md` file. It explained what information I should include.

I used these suggestions to explain how and where I used AI during my work.

## What I learned

Using AI helped me organise my ideas, improve my website plan and understand the assignment. I learned that a website needs clear navigation, good content and correct HTML structure.

I also learned that AI can help me find mistakes and improve my work, but I should always check its suggestions myself to make sure they are correct.
